## About the Role

McKinstry's Software Engineering team builds and maintains the connections that keep our business data moving: integrations, automation, and the pipelines that tie our internal systems together.

As an Integrations Engineer on this team, you'll own the design, development, and maintenance of integrations across McKinstry's application landscape. That means building and supporting API-based connections between internal tools and third-party platforms, managing data flows through our integration layer, and making sure the right data gets to the right place reliably. You'll work closely with both technical and business stakeholders to understand what systems need to talk to each other and why. This is a hands-on role with real ownership — when an integration breaks or a business process gets blocked, you're the one who fixes it.

---
## What You'll Do

### Integration Ownership & Delivery

- Design and implement new integrations across McKinstry's Workday environment, owning work end-to-end from technical design through deployment and ongoing maintenance
- Diagnose and resolve integration failures, data quality issues, and performance problems across the integration portfolio
- Participate in requirements conversations with HR, Finance, and operational stakeholders and translate business needs into reliable, maintainable integration solutions
- Contribute to roadmap and prioritization discussions alongside the engineering manager and functional partners
- Document integrations including integration logic, field mappings, error handling behavior, and runbooks

### Development

- Design, build, and maintain integrations using Workday Studio, Workday Orchestrate, Core Connectors, and EIBs depending on complexity and use case
- Work with Workday APIs, both REST and SOAP/XML, to connect Workday to internal systems and third-party platforms
- Use Workday RAAS and custom reports as integration data sources
- Write Python and scripting solutions to automate, transform, or move data where native Workday tooling doesn't reach
- Handle XML and XSLT transformations within Studio-based integrations
- Build and maintain integrations with third-party systems: benefits carriers, payroll vendors, identity platforms, and internal tools

### Collaboration and Documentation

- Contribute to technical design documents, runbooks, and inline code documentation
- Participate in code reviews and help raise the baseline quality of the team's work
- Partner with business stakeholders to understand operational workflows
- Flag risks early and communicate blockers clearly

---
## What You Bring

### Required

- 3-5 years of professional software engineering or integrations experience, with a focus on enterprise platforms and data flows
- Hands-on experience building and maintaining integrations in Workday Studio, Workday Orchestrate, Core Connectors, and EIBs
- Proficiency in Python or equivalent scripting for data transformation, automation, and integration support tasks
- Familiarity with Workday RAAS and custom reporting as integration data sources
- Ability to write clear technical documentation and communicate findings to non-technical stakeholders

### Highly Preferred

- Hands-on experience with REST API patterns including OAuth 2.0, JSON payloads, and webhook-based flows
- Prior work connecting Workday to third-party systems: benefits carriers, payroll vendors, identity platforms, or internal tools
- Comfort inheriting integrations you didn't build, in environments where documentation is incomplete

## Who You Are

We're looking for someone who:

- **Takes ownership.** When you pick something up, it gets done and done right.
- **Is comfortable with ambiguity.** Existing apps have history. You ask good questions, read the code, and make reasonable calls without waiting for perfect requirements.
- **Thinks in systems.** You understand how a change in one place affects another and you account for it.
- **Communicates clearly.** You can explain a technical problem to a business user and a business problem to a technical teammate.
- **Cares about quality.** You write code others can maintain and documentation others can use.
