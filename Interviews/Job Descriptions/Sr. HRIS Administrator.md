## Here's Where You Come In

McKinstry is reaching the next stage of its Workday journey, and we're looking for a technical expert who is passionate about partnering with teams across organizations to solve problems using technology. This role will deliver product enhancements and new features that align with our 2025 objectives. Successful candidates will have extensive experience in Workday Core HCM, in addition to Workday Studio integrations. Knowledge of Absence, Advanced Compensation, Benefits, and/or Performance modules is a plus.

This position is responsible for the on-going operations of Workday Core HCM, and will focus heavily on business process configuration and integrations. This role collaborates with HR, Technology, and other business stakeholders to design, configure, test, implement, and maintain enhancements to our Workday product.

---

## You're Great At

- Designing and building Workday solutions that are sustainable and scalable, and that create operational efficiencies through automation
- Leading configuration activities for cyclical HR processes, deployment of new modules, improvements, and expanded offerings within Workday HCM platform while ensuring data integrity
- Supporting the People Plan Initiatives through configuration, testing and implementation of enhanced system capabilities
- Providing Tier 2 and 3 technical support for Workday HCM
- Optimally improving Workday while also ensuring data integrity, security, and operational precision
- Developing testing plans for new features, including management of Workday Feature Releases
- Providing support for compliance reporting (EEO, AAP, VETS-4212)

- 3+ years of Workday Report Writing experience (Composite, Matrix, Transposed, nBox, etc.)
