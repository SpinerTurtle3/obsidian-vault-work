Interview questions derived from the [[Interviews/Job Descriptions/Workday Integrations Engineer.md|Workday Integrations Engineer JD]]. Grouped by theme so a scorecard can be built against each section later.

---

## 1. Workday Platform & Tooling

- Walk me through an integration you built using Workday Studio. What made it complex, and why was Studio the right tool for it?
- Tell me about a time you used Workday Orchestrate versus a Core Connector versus an EIB. How do you decide which tool fits a given use case?
- Describe your experience with Workday RAAS and custom reports as integration data sources. What are the limitations you've run into?
- What's your experience level with each: Workday Studio, Orchestrate, Core Connectors, EIBs? Where are you strongest, and where are you still building depth?

## 2. APIs, Data Formats & Scripting

- Describe an integration where you worked with Workday's REST APIs, and one where you worked with SOAP/XML. How did the approaches differ?
- Walk me through an XML/XSLT transformation you built inside a Studio integration. What was the transformation doing, and why couldn't it be handled natively?
- Tell me about a time you used Python (or another scripting language) to move or transform data where native Workday tooling didn't reach. What was the gap, and what did you build?
- What's your experience with OAuth 2.0, JSON payloads, or webhook-based flows in integration work? Give a concrete example.

## 3. Third-Party & Cross-System Integration

- Tell me about an integration you built or maintained connecting Workday to a third-party system — a benefits carrier, payroll vendor, or identity platform. What made it tricky?
- Describe a time an integration with an external vendor broke because of something on their end. How did you diagnose it, and what was your fix or workaround?
- How do you handle situations where a third-party system's API or data contract changes without much notice?

## 4. Integration Ownership, Diagnosis & Reliability

- Walk me through your process when an integration fails in production and a business process is blocked. What do you check first?
- Tell me about a data quality issue you traced back through an integration. How did you find the root cause?
- Describe a performance problem in an integration you owned. What was slow, and how did you fix it?
- Tell me about an integration you inherited that you didn't build, in an environment with incomplete documentation. How did you get up to speed?

## 5. Requirements, Stakeholders & Systems Thinking

- Tell me about a time you translated an HR or Finance business need into a technical integration solution. What did that requirements conversation look like?
- Describe a change you made to an integration that had ripple effects elsewhere in the system landscape. How did you catch it or account for it?
- How do you approach ambiguity when requirements are incomplete or a stakeholder isn't sure exactly what they need yet?

## 6. Ownership & Execution

- Tell me about an integration you owned end-to-end — from technical design through deployment and ongoing maintenance. What was hardest about owning the full lifecycle?
- Describe a time you flagged a risk or blocker early on an integration project. What made you raise it when you did, and how did it land?
- Tell me about a time you picked up integration work that wasn't clearly yours to own. What made you take it, and how did it turn out?

## 7. Collaboration & Communication

- Tell me about a time you explained a technical integration issue to a non-technical HR or Finance stakeholder. How did you adjust your explanation?
- What's your approach to code review for integration work — both giving and receiving feedback?
- Describe a disagreement with a stakeholder or teammate about how an integration should work. How was it resolved?

## 8. Quality & Documentation

- Walk me through how you document an integration — logic, field mappings, error handling, runbooks. What's your standard, and why?
- Tell me about documentation you wrote that you know got used by someone else later, especially during an incident. What made it effective?
- How do you balance shipping an integration quickly against documenting it thoroughly, especially on a lean team?

## 9. Closing / Candidate-Led

- What about this role or team is most appealing to you, and what gives you pause?
- What questions do you have about the integration portfolio, the Workday environment, or how work gets prioritized?
