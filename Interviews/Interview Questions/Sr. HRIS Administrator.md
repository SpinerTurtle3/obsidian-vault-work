Interview questions derived from the [[Interviews/Job Descriptions/Sr. HRIS Administrator.md|Sr. HRIS Administrator JD]]. Grouped by theme so a scorecard can be built against each section later.

---

## 1. Workday Core HCM Configuration

- Walk me through a Workday Core HCM business process you configured end-to-end. What made it sustainable and scalable rather than a one-off fix?
- Tell me about a time you led configuration for a cyclical HR process (open enrollment, annual comp, performance cycle). What was your role, and what went wrong along the way?
- Describe a new module or feature you helped deploy within Workday HCM. How did you approach testing before it went live?
- What's your experience with Absence, Advanced Compensation, Benefits, or Performance modules? Which have you configured directly versus just supported?

## 2. Workday Studio Integrations

- Describe a Workday Studio integration you built or maintained. What was it moving, and why was Studio the right tool?
- Tell me about an inbound or outbound EIB you've supported. What made it reliable, and what broke it when it broke?
- What's your experience with PECI? Walk me through a scenario where you used it.
- How do you decide between Studio, EIB, and other integration approaches for a given requirement?

## 3. Reporting & Data

- Walk me through a Composite, Matrix, Transposed, or nBox report you built. What business question was it answering?
- Tell me about a report that turned out to be wrong or misleading after it was already in use. How did you find it, and what did you fix?
- Describe how you approach data integrity checks when configuring or reporting on Workday data.

## 4. Operations, Support & Troubleshooting

- Walk me through how you triage a Tier 2/3 Workday HCM support ticket. What's your process from "something's broken" to resolution?
- Tell me about a Workday Feature Release you managed. How did you build and execute your testing plan?
- Describe a time a configuration change had an unintended downstream effect on another business process or module. How did you catch it or clean it up?
- Tell me about a time you had to balance "optimally improving Workday" against "ensuring data integrity and operational precision." What did you do differently as a result?

## 5. Compliance & Reporting Obligations

- What's your experience supporting compliance reporting like EEO, AAP, or VETS-4212? Walk me through your role in a reporting cycle.
- Tell me about a time a compliance deadline was at risk. What did you do?
- How do you stay current on compliance reporting requirements that touch HRIS data?

## 6. Stakeholders & Systems Thinking

- Tell me about a time you translated an HR or People Plan initiative into a concrete Workday configuration plan. What did that conversation with stakeholders look like?
- Describe a project where Technology, HR, and another business function all had different priorities for the same Workday change. How did you navigate it?
- How do you approach a request where the "ask" and the actual underlying business need turn out to be different things?

## 7. Ownership & Execution

- Tell me about a Workday project you owned from design through implementation. What made it a success, and what would you do differently?
- Describe a time you demonstrated "curious problem solver" behavior — digging into something beyond what was asked because you sensed there was more to it.
- Tell me about a time your attention to detail caught something others missed in a configuration or report.

## 8. Collaboration & Communication

- Tell me about a time you explained a Workday configuration limitation or tradeoff to a non-technical HR stakeholder. How did you frame it?
- What's your approach to documenting configuration decisions so the next person (or you, in a year) understands why something was built the way it was?
- Describe a disagreement with a stakeholder about how a process should be configured. How was it resolved?

## 9. Closing / Candidate-Led

- What about this role or McKinstry's Workday journey is most appealing to you, and what gives you pause?
- What questions do you have about the current state of our Workday environment, the team, or how priorities get set?
