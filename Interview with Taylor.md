
**1. What does the current state of the data and software org look like, and what's the biggest thing the person in this role needs to fix first?**  
Forces honesty. You learn if this is a "build" situation, a "stabilize" situation, or a political mess someone else left behind.

**2. Where is AI on the roadmap, and has leadership aligned on what success actually looks like there?**  
Credit unions are all over the map on AI maturity. This tells you if you'd be educating the C-suite from scratch or executing on a real strategy.

**3. What's the relationship between this role and the CTO/CIO, and how are technology investment decisions made?**  
Scope and authority questions matter a lot at VP level. You want to know if you'd have real influence or be a delivery manager with a fancy title.

**4. What's the primary driver for this hire - is this a backfill, a newly elevated role, or a response to something specific happening in the business?**  
The answer tells you a lot. "We're growing" is different from "the last person didn't work out" is different from "we just committed to a major transformation."

**5. (Optional closer) What would make the first 90 days successful in your mind?**  
Good for gauging how tactical vs. strategic their expectations are, and whether there's alignment at the top.
